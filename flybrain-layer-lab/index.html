    Inside this specimen

    This puzzle uses real reconstructed neurons from FlyWire FAFB v783. These are skeletons traced from electron-microscopy data, not raw grayscale microscope images.

    160sampled neurons
    15geometric slabs
    18.77 µmslab thickness

    Every neuron is cut from a single continuous reconstruction. At the correct joins, its endpoints coincide exactly. This sample retains the published skeleton detail. Line widths and colours aid visibility; they do not represent actual neuron diameter or microscope staining.

    The outer brain surface is a processed tissue-mask mesh transformed into FlyWire coordinates. It is an anatomical guide. The 15 slabs are much thicker than individual EM sections; they show a projection of branching inside each slab.

    Which 160 neurons?

    These are individual neurons, not 160 synaptic connections or a selected circuit. The sample contains 84 optic, 38 central, 33 sensory, 3 ascending and 2 descending neurons.

    To spread the sample across the brain, annotation anchor positions were grouped into an 8-by-6 grid along the lateral (x) and scan-depth (z) axes. Four neurons were randomly selected from each of 40 occupied bins, using a fixed seed of 783. This is a spatial teaching sample, not a representative statistical sample or a connected circuit.
